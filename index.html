Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sprint models, pricing milestones, code ownership, and post-launch guarantee.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Landing pages and starter MVPs are typically delivered within 1 to 2 weeks. Full-scale SaaS web applications and headless e-commerce platforms take approximately 4 to 8 weeks, broken down into iterative agile sprints with live staging deployments every single Friday.

How do pricing milestones and payments work?

We operate on a transparent milestone-based payment schedule (e.g., 30% upfront deposit, 40% upon staging milestone approval, and 30% upon final production launch). All payments are secured with formal invoices, contracts, and digital receipts.

Do I own 100% of the code and intellectual property?

Yes, absolutely 100%. Upon final project sign-off and payment, all copyrights, GitHub repositories, Figma source files, database schemas, and intellectual property are completely transferred to your organization with zero ongoing licensing fees.

What post-launch support and warranty are included?

Every project comes with standard 14 to 30 days of complimentary bug-fix warranty and dedicated technical support. We also provide ongoing monthly retainer packages for continuous feature development, security patches, and cloud monitoring.

How do we communicate throughout the project?

We establish a dedicated private Slack or Discord channel with your team, alongside weekly Zoom / Google Meet check-ins. You receive video walkthroughs of completed features and 24/7 access to live staging environments.

Can you work with our existing codebase or legacy systems?

Yes! We routinely audit, refactor, and integrate modern microservices into established systems, whether migrating from older monoliths to Next.js or connecting complex REST/GraphQL APIs with existing databases.
